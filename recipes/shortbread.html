Shortbread

Description

This is a quick and easy shortbread recipe with choco chunks. Total time from start to finish, including baking in the oven, is less than 1 hour. Shortbread is best enjoyed with tea, coffee or milk.

Ingredients

  • 100 g granulated sugar
  • 200 g butter
  • 300 g all purpose flour
  • 75g dark chocolate
  • pinch of salt

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, add the flour, sugar and a pinch of salt, then quickly mix the ingredients with a whisk.
  2. Add the butter and start mixing and rubbing the ingredients between your hands until they resemble breadrumbs, then press together until it starts to form a dough.
    • Tip: If your dough is too crumbly and wont hold together add a teaspoon of water.
  3. Chop the chocolate, add it to your dough and mix it in until well combined.
  4. Tip out the dough onto your work surface, roll it into a sausage shape, then cover and chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.
    • Chilling will make it easier to cut the dough and reduce spreading out while baking. However, if you are in a hurry you can skip chilling.
  5. Take the dough out of the fridge and slice it into pieces, however thick you like.
  6. Place the pieces onto a lined baking tray and bake at 170 °C until golden brown on the edges (20-25 minutes or 15-20 minutes if you didnt chill your dough).
  7. Remove from the oven and let the cookies cool on the tray for 10 minutes, then carefully transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
