High Protein Pizza Dough

Description

This high-protein pizza dough is a game-changer for quick, healthy homemade pizza nights. Made with self-rising flour, Greek yogurt, and unflavored protein powder, it comes together in minutes with no yeast and no rising time. The result is a soft, chewy crust with crispy edges that holds up well to toppings.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup self-rising flour
  • 1 scoop unflavored protein powder
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt or high protein Greek yogurt
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder, Italian herbs, or salt (optional)

Steps

  1. Whisk self-rising flour and protein powder together in a bowl until evenly combined. Add Greek yogurt and stir with a spoon or fork until a shaggy dough forms.
  2. Knead lightly by hand on a lightly floured surface until smooth and not sticky, 30 to 60 seconds. If too dry or crumbly, add yogurt 1 teaspoon at a time. If too sticky, add a sprinkle of flour. Let dough rest for 10 minutes.
  3. Roll or press out on parchment paper into your desired shape and thickness. Dust with a little flour if sticky.
  4. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C).
  5. Bake plain crust on a pizza pan or stone in the preheated oven for 5 to 7 minutes. Add sauce, cheese, and toppings, then bake until golden and crispy, 8 to 12 minutes more. Enjoy!
